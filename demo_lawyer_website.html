About

A career built on
principled advocacy.

I established my practice in 2012 after completing my LLB from Campus Law Centre, Delhi University, and an LLM in Criminal Law from NLSIU Bangalore. I appear regularly before the Delhi High Court and district courts across NCR.

My practice is built on one principle: every client deserves to understand their situation fully — and to feel genuinely heard.

Practice Areas

Areas of expertise

⚖️

Criminal Defence

Bail applications, trial representation, appeals. Experienced in IPC offences, white-collar crime, and NDPS matters before Delhi courts.

🏛️

Family Law

Divorce, judicial separation, child custody, maintenance, and matrimonial property disputes. Handled with sensitivity and discretion.

📜

Civil Litigation

Property disputes, injunctions, contract matters, recovery suits and execution proceedings before civil courts in Delhi-NCR.

🏠

Property & Real Estate

Title disputes, builder-buyer matters, tenancy law, DDA allotment issues and succession / probate in relation to immovable property.

Domestic Violence

Consumer Disputes

Representation before District and State Consumer Commissions for deficiency of service, insurance disputes and banking complaints.
